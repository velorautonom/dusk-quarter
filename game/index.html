DUSK QUARTER

round-based tactical FPS

You run with the Attackers. Take a site, plant the charge and hold it — or wipe the Wardens out. Earn money every round, spend it in the armoury, win five rounds to take the match.

  • WASD move · Ctrl crouch · Space jump
  • Mouse look · LMB fire · R reload
  • E plant / defuse (hold) · G grenade · 1–4 weapons
  • B armoury (buy window)
